SLIDE 2: INTRODUCTION & BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slide Title: Introduction & Social Context
Bullet Points:
• Rapid Mobile Penetration: Rural India has experienced exponential 4G/5G mobile internet adoption and smartphone ownership.
• The Missing Link: Despite internet connectivity, rural students primarily utilize smartphones for social media and video streaming, unaware of high-quality educational portals.
• The Education Divide: Urban students benefit from peer mentorship, coaching, and awareness of platforms like W3Schools, GeeksforGeeks, HackerRank, and SWAYAM.
• EduBridge Mission: To bridge this digital information gap by creating a free, bilingual discovery hub connecting rural students directly to global learning resources.

Speaker Notes:
"While hardware and connectivity have reached rural villages, digital educational awareness has lagged behind. Many students do not know that platforms like W3Schools, LeetCode, or Harvard's CS50 exist and are completely free. EduBridge was born to solve this exact problem."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3: PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slide Title: Problem Statement & Rural Challenges
Bullet Points:
• Information Asymmetry: Millions of free educational tutorials, textbooks, and compiler tools exist online, but rural students do not know where to start.
• High Financial Barriers: Commercial coaching institutes charge heavy fees (₹20,000 to ₹50,000) for basic programming lessons that are freely available online.
• Language Hesitation: Technical English creates fear and hesitation among rural regional-medium students.
• Hardware Misconception: A widespread belief that learning programming strictly requires an expensive laptop, preventing mobile-only learners from writing code.
• Lack of Structured Roadmaps: Confusion regarding skill sequences for career roles like Full Stack, Python, or Data Analytics.

Speaker Notes:
"Through our rural survey, we identified four major bottlenecks: lack of awareness, fear of English, the false belief that programming requires an expensive PC, and absence of mentorship. Students waste money or give up because they lack guidance."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 4: PROJECT OBJECTIVES & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slide Title: Project Objectives & Scope
Bullet Points:
• Centralized Discovery: Build a single-window portal cataloging 47+ verified, genuine educational platforms.
• Bilingual Orientation: Provide parallel Telugu (తెలుగు) summaries, explanations, and advice for beginners.
• Academic Integration: Organize 12 foundational B.Tech CSE/IT subjects with curated lecture playlists (Gate Smashers, Abdul Bari, Neso Academy).
• Transparency in Pricing: Clearly classify resources into 100% Free, Free Audit, and Govt-sponsored (NPTEL, Infosys Springboard, IBM).
• Career Roadmaps: Deliver 9 step-by-step career path guides with beginner project ideas.
• Scope Boundary: EduBridge is an awareness & resource discovery gateway, not a replacement LMS. It redirects students directly to official providers.

Speaker Notes:
"Our primary objective is to empower rural students with self-paced learning. EduBridge does not duplicate external platforms; instead, it organizes, clarifies, and points students directly to official verified portals with Telugu guidance."

SLIDE 5: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slide Title: System Architecture & Workflow
Bullet Points:
• 3-Tier Web Architecture: Presentation Layer (HTML5/CSS3/JS), Application Server (Node.js/Express), and Data Persistence (Embedded SQLite).
• High-Performance Embedded Database: Built-in `node:sqlite` (DatabaseSync) providing zero-latency queries without separate database server processes.
• Stateless Token Security: JWT (JSON Web Tokens) with HTTP-only cookies for student authentication and role-based access control.
• Responsive Client: Mobile-first responsive CSS tailored for budget 4G smartphones and low bandwidth usage.
• Direct Traffic Redirection: Tracks student visits and redirects directly to genuine external platforms in isolated browser tabs.

Speaker Notes:
"EduBridge is designed to run efficiently on both desktop and low-end mobile devices. The architecture uses Express for REST APIs and Node's modern built-in SQLite engine, ensuring rapid startup and zero external database overhead."

SLIDE 6: CORE MODULES & FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slide Title: Core Modules of EduBridge
Bullet Points:
• 1. Global Resource Catalog (`explore.html`): Real-time search, multi-filter by Category, Difficulty Level, and Pricing Model.

• 7. Career Roadmaps (`career-guidance.html`): 9 software job roadmaps with required skills, practice platforms, and project ideas.

Speaker Notes:
"EduBridge covers the entire student journey: from learning their first line of Python in their phone browser, to cracking semester exams, obtaining free IBM/NPTEL certificates, and preparing for campus placement aptitude tests."

SLIDE 7: KEY RESEARCH INNOVATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slide Title: Special Features for Rural Beginners
Bullet Points:
• Bilingual Toggle Engine (English & Telugu): Instant one-click language switcher without page reload, providing native Telugu explanations for complex concepts.
• Digital Literacy Handbook (`digital-literacy.html`): 11 guided lessons:
  - Setting up professional Gmail accounts
  - Video data saving (360p/480p vs 1080p)
  - Coding on mobile phones with ₹30 OTG keyboards
  - Identifying genuine government portals (.ac.in / .gov.in) vs scam sites
  - Cyber hygiene, strong passwords, and OTP security.
• Zero-Hardware Barrier: Actively promotes browser-based cloud IDEs (Replit, Programiz) so students don't need a laptop.

Speaker Notes:
"Our research highlighted that rural students needed more than just links; they needed basic digital orientation. Our 11 Digital Literacy modules teach students how to save mobile data, create professional emails, and stay safe from cyber fraud."

SLIDE 9: DATABASE DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slide Title: Database Schema & Entity Relationships
Bullet Points:
• 1. `users` Table: User ID, Name, Email, Password Hash (Bcrypt), Role (Admin/Student), Village, College, Study Level, Language, Interests.
• 2. `resources` Table: Resource ID, Name, URL, Category, Subcategories, Skill Level, Pricing, Description, Telugu Description, Featured Flag, Click Count.
• 3. `bookmarks` Table: Composite Unique (user_id, resource_id) with cascading deletion.
• 4. `visits` Table: Tracking student redirection history and timestamp logs.
• 5. `feedback` Table: Student submissions, broken link reports, message content, and status tracking (pending/reviewed/resolved).

Speaker Notes:
"The database schema is lightweight and relational, enforcing foreign key integrity and password hashing using bcrypt. The embedded SQLite database ensures instant initialization and portable deployments."

Bullet Points:

  - HTML5 & Responsive Semantic Elements

  - Bcrypt.js for secure cryptographic password hashing
• Database:
  - Node.js Embedded SQLite (`node:sqlite` DatabaseSync)
• Deployment:
  - Cloud-ready via Render.com / GitHub version control

Speaker Notes:
"Our stack is chosen for maximum speed and zero bloat. By avoiding heavy frontend frameworks like React or Angular, pages load in milliseconds even over rural 3G/4G connections with low bandwidth consumption."
