⚡ Speed Math & Placement Formula Cheatsheet

Memorize these key relations to solve aptitude questions in under 45 seconds during online tests:

⏱️ Time, Speed & Distance

  • Speed = Distance / Time
  • km/h to m/s: Multiply by 5/18
  • m/s to km/h: Multiply by 18/5
  • Relative speed (same direction) = u - v
  • Relative speed (opposite direction) = u + v

💼 Time & Work

  • If A can do a job in 'n' days, 1 day work = 1/n
  • If A & B work together: Time = (A × B) / (A + B)
  • M1 × D1 × H1 / W1 = M2 × D2 × H2 / W2
  • Pipes & Cisterns: Inflow (+) and Leakage (-)

💰 Profit, Loss & Percentage

  • Gain% = (Gain × 100) / Cost Price
  • Loss% = (Loss × 100) / Cost Price
  • Selling Price = CP × (100 + Gain%) / 100
  • Successive Discounts: a + b - (a × b)/100

🎲 Probability & Permutations

  • nPr = n! / (n - r)! (Arrangement matters)
  • nCr = n! / [r! × (n - r)!] (Selection only)
  • P(Event) = Favorable Outcomes / Total Outcomes
  • P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)
