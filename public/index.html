KEY RESEARCH MODULE

Digital Literacy Awareness for Rural Villages

Many rural students have smartphones and internet data but lack guidance on how to harness them for education. Our digital literacy module provides clear, step-by-step guidance in simple English and Telugu (తెలుగు).

  • How to set up a professional Gmail account for college & jobs
  • How to search for educational material without wasting mobile data
  • How to learn coding online without an expensive laptop (Replit / online editors)
  • Basic online safety, fake website identification, and password privacy

ACADEMIC RESEARCH & COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT

A STUDY ON DIGITAL EDUCATION AWARENESS IN RURAL VILLAGES

Read Full Study Details ➔

In contemporary India, millions of high-quality educational resources—such as W3Schools, GeeksforGeeks, HackerRank, freeCodeCamp, NPTEL, and SWAYAM—are accessible 100% free of charge. However, a major awareness gap persists among students residing in rural villages due to lack of exposure, language hesitation, and absence of unified guidance.

🎯 Project Objective Increase awareness of digital education by centralizing useful websites and providing actionable roadmaps.
🛡️ Scope & Ethics Strictly an awareness and discovery gateway. Does not host fake exams or duplicate external platforms.
🌱 Social Impact Bridge the rural-urban technical divide and inspire self-paced learning and career upward mobility.
