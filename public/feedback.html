Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries rural students ask about digital education:

Do I need an expensive laptop to start learning coding? ▼
No! You can write and run C, Python, Java, and HTML code straight from your mobile browser using platforms like Programiz Online Compiler or Replit without downloading software. A standard smartphone with 4G internet is sufficient to master basic programming.
Are all the websites listed on EduBridge really free? ▼
Yes! Every platform on EduBridge is vetted. Platforms like W3Schools, GeeksforGeeks, freeCodeCamp, IBM SkillsBuild, and Infosys Springboard provide 100% free learning. On Coursera, you can use the "Audit" option to learn for free.
Can I really get a software job by self-studying from these websites? ▼
Absolutely! Many engineering graduates from rural polytechnics and Tier-3 colleges secure IT positions at TCS, Infosys, and product startups by practicing problems on HackerRank and LeetCode, building portfolio projects, and following our structured Career Roadmaps.
How do I get free certificates on Coursera? ▼
Next to the "Enroll" button on any Coursera course, click the small link labeled "Financial Aid Available". Fill out the application explaining that you are a rural Indian student committed to acquiring skills. Coursera approves eligible student applications within 15 days at zero charge.
How does EduBridge help my college exam preparation? ▼
Visit our B.Tech Subjects section. We have organized top playlists from Gate Smashers, Abdul Bari, and Neso Academy that break down university syllabus units (OS, DBMS, CN, Automata, Compiler) into simple 15-minute conceptual videos.
