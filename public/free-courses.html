Free Online Courses & Certifications

World-class companies (IBM, Infosys, Microsoft, Cisco) and governments (IITs, Ministry of Education) offer thousands of free courses with verified digital certificates. Learn how to identify 100% free offerings vs audit-only options.

🎓 Understanding Course Types & Certification Charges

To avoid confusion, EduBridge categorizes all online learning platforms into three transparent tiers:

🟢 100% Free Learning & Certificate

Platforms: IBM SkillsBuild, Infosys Springboard, Great Learning Academy, Kaggle Learn, CS50.
Cost: ₹0 for entire course videos, assignments, and verified digital certificates/badges.

🔵 100% Free Learning + Optional Govt. Exam

Platforms: NPTEL & SWAYAM (Govt. of India).
Cost: All IIT video lectures and assignments are 100% free. Optional in-person proctored exam fee (₹1,000) only if credit transfer is required.

🟡 Free Audit + Financial Aid Available

Platforms: Coursera & edX.
Cost: Click "Audit Course" to access all video lectures and readings for ₹0. Rural students can submit a 2-paragraph "Financial Aid" application for 100% free certificates.

💡 గ్రామీణ విద్యార్థుల కోసం చిట్కా (How to Get Free Certificates):

1. ఇన్ఫోసిస్ స్ప్రింగ్‌బోర్డ్ (Infosys Springboard) లో మీ ఈమెయిల్‌తో ఉచితంగా రిజిస్టర్ అవ్వండి. ఇందులో వేల కొద్దీ ఐటీ కోర్సులు మరియు ఇన్ఫోసిస్ సర్టిఫికేట్లు 100% ఉచితం.
