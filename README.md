# EduBridge: One Platform. Unlimited Learning Opportunities.
### A Study on Digital Education Awareness in Rural Villages

**EduBridge** is a modern, responsive, and user-friendly educational resource discovery web application developed for the academic research project: **"A STUDY ON DIGITAL EDUCATION AWARENESS IN RURAL VILLAGES"**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective & Purpose

The primary objective is to increase awareness of digital education among rural village students by introducing them to useful online educational websites, free learning resources, coding practice platforms, subject-wise tutorials, online courses, and career development opportunities.

Many rural students possess smartphones and 4G internet access but are unaware of world-class free educational platforms such as **W3Schools, GeeksforGeeks, HackerRank, CodeChef, freeCodeCamp, NPTEL, SWAYAM, and Coursera**. EduBridge brings all these resources together into one centralized, bilingual gateway.

> **Scope Note:** EduBridge is strictly an educational awareness and resource discovery gateway. It is **NOT** a college management system. It does not host exams or fake compilers; it organizes verified educational resources and redirects students directly to their original websites.

---

## 🌟 Key Application Features

1. **Bilingual Support (English & Telugu - తెలుగు):**
   - Instant header language switcher.
   - Comprehensive Telugu guidance notes for rural beginners.

2. **Homepage:**
   - Prominent search bar with live topic suggestions.
   - Featured popular platforms and category tiles.
   - Digital literacy awareness spotlight.
   - Official study citation and research overview.

3. **Global Explore & Multi-Filter Directory (`explore.html`):**
   - Real-time search by website name, programming language, subject, or career path.
   - Filter by Category, Skill Level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced), and Pricing (100% Free, Free Audit, Freemium).
   - Topic chip filters and sorting options.

4. **Programming Learning Section (`programming.html`):**
   - 12 individual categories: C, C++, Java, Python, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, SQL, PHP, Data Structures and Algorithms, Web Development, Database Management.
   - Verified platforms: W3Schools, GeeksforGeeks, JavaTpoint, Programiz, freeCodeCamp, TutorialsPoint, MDN Web Docs, LearnCpp, SQLZoo, The Odin Project.

5. **Coding Practice Section (`coding-practice.html`):**
   - Platforms: HackerRank, CodeChef, LeetCode, HackerEarth, Codeforces, Coding Ninjas Code360, Exercism, Project Euler, Replit.
   - Categorized by skill level and problem-solving focus.

6. **B.Tech Subject-Wise Learning (`btech-subjects.html`):**
   - 12 Core CSE/IT Subjects: Data Structures, DBMS, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Automata Theory (FLAT), Compiler Design, Software Engineering, AI, Machine Learning, Computer Organization, Engineering Mathematics, Cybersecurity.
   - Detailed concept breakdowns, semester exam tips, and curated video playlists (Gate Smashers, Abdul Bari, Neso Academy, Jenny's Lectures).

7. **Free Online Courses & Certifications (`free-courses.html`):**
   - Transparent pricing breakdown: 100% Free with Certificate (IBM SkillsBuild, Infosys Springboard), Free Audit (Coursera/edX), and Govt. of India Sponsored (NPTEL, SWAYAM).
   - How to apply for Coursera 100% Financial Aid.

8. **Aptitude & Reasoning (`aptitude.html`):**
   - Quantitative Math, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Ability, Placement & Interview preparation (IndiaBIX, PrepInsta, GeeksforGeeks).
   - Built-in Speed Math formula cheatsheet.

9. **Career Roadmaps (`career-guidance.html`):**
   - 9 complete step-by-step roadmaps: Full Stack Developer, Java Developer, Python Developer, Data Analyst, Data Scientist, AI/ML Engineer, Cybersecurity Analyst, Cloud Engineer, Software Tester (QA).

10. **Digital Literacy Awareness Handbook (`digital-literacy.html`):**
    - 11 guided modules in simple English & Telugu on email setup, using internet data prudently, online coding without a PC, video speed controls, and identifying fake websites.

11. **Student Learning Dashboard (`dashboard.html`):**
    - Welcome greeting with village and college details.
    - Saved bookmarks with instant removal or launch.
    - Recently visited platforms timeline.
    - Personalized recommendations matching selected interests.
    - Profile & language preference editor.

12. **Administrator Console (`admin.html`):**
    - Add, edit, and delete educational platforms with English & Telugu descriptions.
    - Real-time platform analytics (resources, users, visits, feedback).
    - Manage student website suggestions and broken link reports.

13. **Student Feedback & FAQs (`feedback.html`):**
    - Suggest useful educational websites, report broken links, request topics.
    - Interactive FAQ accordion answering rural student questions.

14. **Official Research Study Page (`about.html`):**
    - Official title: "A STUDY ON DIGITAL EDUCATION AWARENESS IN RURAL VILLAGES".
    - Problem statement, challenges, objectives, social impact, expected outcomes, and future scope.
